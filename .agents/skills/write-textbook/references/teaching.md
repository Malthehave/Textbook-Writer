# Teach so the learner can follow

Begin with the reader's actual knowledge, a concrete need and an example. Introduce formal
language after its meaning has something to attach to. Explain why each operation follows.

Before unfamiliar notation, label relevant direction, order, place values, units, axes,
indices and symbols. Walk through a labelled example. Contrast a plausible novice reading
when it changes the result. Choose examples that expose ambiguity instead of hiding it.


Plan illustrations around conceptual obstacles. Give each a question to answer, useful
labels and an explanation in prose. Several compact illustrations can help a short book;
there is no arbitrary one-figure-per-chapter cap. Avoid decorative diagrams and quotas.

Use connected prose, meaningful transitions and varied rhythm. Preserve one anchor example
and deepen it across sections. Explain analogies' mapping and relevant limits. Exercises
progress from interpretation to application and transfer; their answers must not be the
first place a prerequisite gets explained.

Reader review scores these dimensions independently from 1–5:
narrative_coherence, explanatory_depth, paragraph_flow, sentence_rhythm,
concept_scaffolding, example_continuity, practice_progression, voice_consistency.
All must reach 4 for approval. Accurate but unexplained notation is a scaffolding/depth
defect. Locate evidence and request a concrete repair, not vague polish or extra diagrams.

## Learning architecture before prose

Derive the curriculum from the confirmed goal and researched evidence. Plan the dependency
chain from what this reader knows to the capability they want. For each chapter state the
central question, prerequisite concepts, conceptual obstacle, intuition bridge, worked
example progression, misconceptions, evidence, progressive practice and handoff to the
next chapter. These are authoring notes, not repetitive learner-facing headings.

Use an authentic running system or project when it serves the goal. Teach a mechanism on
a small tractable example, then scale it to the real system. Verify named model versions,
configurations and technical claims against primary sources. Distinguish facts, deliberate
teaching simplifications, estimates with assumptions, vendor claims and unknowns. Compare
alternatives through common criteria and consequences, not separate company profiles.

Personalize the route, examples, pace and scaffolding; preserve factual standards and
intellectual depth. Beginner prerequisites call for more explanation, not less ambition.
Avoid remedial material the learner already understands, but never infer domain knowledge
solely from their education or job title.

## Review against the actual learning promise

The reader editor must receive the learning brief and its personalization decisions. Check
that the book actually follows them: sufficient depth, helpful visual explanations,
prerequisites taught in time, meaningful example continuity and practice that demonstrates
the promised capability. Locate concrete evidence for scores. A technically accurate,
well-formatted summary that fails the learning promise requires revision. Self-assigned
scores and automated checks are evidence to assess, not a guarantee of teaching quality.
