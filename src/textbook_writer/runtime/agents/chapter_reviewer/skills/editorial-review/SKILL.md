# Textbook editorial review

Accuracy is necessary but not sufficient. Approve prose only when a reader can follow its
argument without reconstructing missing connections.

## Review order

Review in two passes. A line edit cannot rescue a broken structure.

### 1. Structural edit

Check whether:

- the opening creates a concrete need and activates `builds_on`;
- the chapter consistently pursues its `central_question`;
- sections form a causal or logical progression rather than a topic inventory;
- the `anchor_example` accumulates meaning instead of appearing once and disappearing;
- explanations arrive before exercises or conclusions depend on them;
- figures are prepared, interpreted, and integrated into the argument;
- the summary resolves the chapter and delivers the planned `hands_off`;
- prior material is used without being needlessly retaught.

Reject a chapter that is factually correct but assembled from standalone mini-articles.

### 2. Line and paragraph edit

Read the learner-facing prose in rendered order. Check whether:

- paragraphs develop ideas through mechanism, evidence, consequence, or contrast;
- transitions express real relationships instead of merely announcing the next topic;
- sentence lengths and structures vary naturally;
- strings of short assertions have not replaced explanation;
- dense bullet lists are limited to truly parallel material or procedures;
- terminology and voice remain stable;
- examples are interpreted rather than left to speak for themselves;
- analogies preserve the relevant relationship, map their parts, state important limits,
  and yield to exact terminology;
- repeated definitions, summaries, and source disclaimers have been removed.

Do not request vague polish. Every revision note must quote or precisely locate evidence,
explain the reader-facing defect, and state a concrete editorial change.

## Check hidden prerequisites and notation

At the first use of an unfamiliar representation, check whether the agreed reader can
interpret its symbols, order/direction, units, indices, and labels from preceding prose.
Try a plausible novice reading: would a reversed order, unintroduced convention, or
missing intermediate step produce a different result? If so, require a labelled example
and explanation before that representation is used. An example whose symmetry masks
the ambiguity is insufficient evidence of clear teaching.

Treat an accurate but unexplained conversion as a concept-scaffolding or explanatory-depth
defect, and request a concrete repair under the existing scorecard. Recommend a table or
diagram when spatial relationships are the missing explanation. Do not impose a diagram
quota or introduce prerequisites unrelated to the reader's goals.

## Machine-like prose signals

Request revision for recurring patterns such as:

- one- or two-sentence paragraphs with the rhythm of bullets;
- repeated “X is… / X enables… / X matters…” paragraph openings;
- section introductions that announce coverage instead of beginning the explanation;
- generic transitions (“Additionally,” “Another key aspect”) with no logical connection;
- interchangeable examples that never accumulate into a worked understanding;
- excessive headings, labels, checklists, or recap boxes interrupting the argument;
- unsupported adjectives in place of mechanism;
- abrupt shifts from sourced claim to sourced claim without synthesis.

A single short paragraph or list can be appropriate. Judge the accumulated reading
experience, not isolated surface statistics.

## Complete reader-experience pass

For the complete-book review, read the chapters in plan order and inspect the full
manuscript as one work. Review:

- whether chapter questions collectively resolve the book's learning goal;
- whether openings and conclusions create genuine handoffs;
- whether established concepts remain stable and later chapters use them;
- whether explanations or analogies repeat without adding depth;
- whether the running example progresses rather than resetting;
- whether voice, assumed reader knowledge, and prose density remain consistent;
- whether the final chapter synthesizes or transfers the book's accumulated capability.

Put each requested change on the chapter where the reader experiences the defect. Prefer
the smallest revision that repairs the book-level arc. Score narrative coherence,
explanatory depth, paragraph flow, sentence rhythm, concept scaffolding, example continuity,
practice progression, and voice consistency independently from 1–5. Any dimension below 4
requires revision with precise evidence. Approve only when all scores are at least 4 and no
remaining note would materially improve coherence, comprehension, or human readability.
